FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you guarantee sales or leads?

No. We set clear objectives and work to improve performance, but outcomes depend on your offer, market, pricing, creative, landing page and competition.

Is your fee the same as my ad budget?

No. The management fee covers our services. Advertising spend is paid separately to the platform.

What is ROAS?

Attributed revenue divided by ad spend. ₦500,000 from ₦100,000 spent is 5.0x, which does not mean ₦400,000 profit.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We receive only the permissions needed to do the work.

How soon will I see results?

Campaigns generate data soon after launch, but reliable conclusions depend on conversion volume, tracking quality and sales cycle.

Can you manage my existing campaigns?

Yes, after an account review and agreement on scope.
